*Draft for owner approval. Last updated: [DATE].*

## Bubbl Pro
You can get a **full refund within 7 days of purchase**. No reason needed.

- **Bought on the web (Razorpay):** email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your account email and payment ID. Once we approve it, the money normally returns to your original payment method in 5–7 working days; the bank's timing can vary.
- **Bought in the Android app (Google Play):** ask through Google Play or email us. Google's refund process and timing apply.

Your Pro access ends when the refund is made. After 7 days, purchases aren't refundable, unless the law requires it or you were charged by mistake.

## Charged twice?
Email us with both payment IDs, and we'll refund the extra payment in full.

Refund terms for centres are set in the centre's agreement with us.
