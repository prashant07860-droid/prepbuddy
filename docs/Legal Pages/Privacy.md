# Privacy Policy

*Draft for owner approval. Last updated: [DATE].*

This policy explains what Bubbl collects and why. Bubbl is run by Prashant Rana, an individual based in India ("we"). It's written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

## What we collect
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash) or Google sign-in | To run your account |
| Display name | Shown on leaderboards instead of your email |
| Phone number, only if you add it | To contact you about your account |
| Exam, language and target year | To set up your practice |
| Answers, scores, time per question, streaks, XP and bookmarks | To run practice and show your progress |
| Error reports and notes you send | To fix questions |
| Purchase records: amount, date, payment reference | To give you Pro and keep receipts |
| App usage events and crash reports | To fix problems and improve Bubbl |
| Device notification token, if you allow reminders | To send your streak reminder |
| Waitlist name, email and optional phone | To tell you when Bubbl launches |

**We don't collect or store your card, UPI or bank details.** Razorpay or Google Play handles those. We don't sell your data, and Bubbl has no ads.

## Who we share it with
Only the services needed to run Bubbl:
- Emergent (app hosting and database)
- Razorpay and Google Play, with RevenueCat (payments)
- [EMAIL PROVIDER] (account and receipt emails)
- Google (sign-in, if you use it, and waitlist forms)
- Sentry (crash reports)

We may also share data if the law requires it.

If you join through a coaching centre, the centre can see your name, test results and progress in that batch.

## Where and how long
Our providers may store data outside India. We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, except records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

## Your choices and rights
You can:
- see and correct your details in the app
- hide yourself from leaderboards in Settings
- turn off reminders
- ask for a copy of your data
- withdraw consent or [delete your account](/delete-account)
- raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India

**Grievance contact:** [NAME], ask@zeppmedia.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.

## Age
Bubbl is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we'll delete the account.

We'll post changes here and update the date above.
