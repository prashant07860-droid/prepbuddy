# Delete Your Bubbl Account

*Draft for owner approval.*

**In the app:** go to Settings → Delete my account → confirm. It takes effect straight away.

**Without the app:** email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account's email with the subject "Delete my account". We'll finish within 7 days.

**What's deleted:** your name, email, phone, password, attempts, scores, streaks, bookmarks and leaderboard entries.

**What's kept:** payment records, for as long as tax and accounting laws require (usually up to 8 years), and error reports with your name removed.

Deleting your account also ends any remaining Pro period, so request any refund you're due first. See the [Refund Policy](/refund).
